# Fictional contrast case: neighbourhood notices
A team first requests a mobile app. The author clarifies the goal is reliable notice delivery even to members without smartphones. The conventional route changes requirements; the Book route also preserves the reason, registers alternatives and declines to treat an installed app as proof of delivery.

Negative control: a newly found limitation of email requires reviewing the delivery decision but *not* an unrelated colour preference. Before acting define a user-facing criterion, permission to contact testers, and the evidence source. Synthetic illustration; no real users or deliveries were tested.
